Its been amusing to watch the tech leaders bend the knee and kiss the ring with the pronouncement that "Henceforth what was previously known as artificial intelligence shall be known as superintelligence" .. why?.. because artificial sounds .. well.. artificial.. sigh..

Satya Nadella used the term in his recent missive, which in a blinding case of enlightened self-interest now that they arent tightly aligned with OpenAI, has Microsoft on to the path of safety and regulation. Brad Smith joining in, saying AI is less regulated than milk. Of course he should have said "superintelligence" but hey it needs focus and practice to keep up with the emperors naming edicts.

The problem we have currently is that its hard to get a calm, informed, objective viewpoint because of the overlay of economic, competitive and geopolitical pressures. So expect the swirl to continue for a while.

In other news we are starting to see European(mistral) and US(reflection (rumored to be on the Nvidia acquihire shopping list)) based open models starting to get competitive with the chinese models. And lots of competition developing in the decision model space now that Jev has made it popular. Microsoft just announced their offering.. initially based on chinese opensource models but soon to be based on their own proprietary models.

The space is never dull.
