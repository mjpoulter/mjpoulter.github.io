The last couple of weeks have shown that Nvidia are compeletly focused on retaining their position at the top of the AI infrastructure ecosystem. Lots of investments that are entirely aimed at keeping folks beholden to them. And in parallel they have started to make more software and model building bets with last weeks investment/licensing of IP from poolside and rumored investment in perplexity. They are capturing the lions share of the AI markets profits and sensibly putting it to work to ensure their future success in an aggressive way vs passively trying to keep competitors at bay. Jensen is clearly going to continue to leverage their dominant position to stay dominant. Going to be interesting to see where the competition comes from, coz come it will. The high margins are way too attractive for folks to stand idly by. But as we've seen with ARM and Intel before them, its hard to dislodge the dominant provider in the chip space. Lets see, if ARM or AMD or Intel or Google or Broadcom etc have something up their sleeves.
