Its becoming increasingly clear that the market is now satiated on GenAI capital asks. The cost of credit default swaps for coreweave debt has got to distressed levels and their stock is getting hit hard. Its probably the case that they are less of a risk than some of the newer entrants, but they are being seen as the indicator for concerns. And increasingly folks are looking at the backstops provided by Nvidia, Google, Microsoft etc and wondering what the hit to them will be if there are defaults. We are also seeing some speculative efforts falling apart, such as Microsoft pulling out of a deal in the UK because the power isnt going to be available any time soon. So lots of concerns.

The question on "is it a bubble" is i think that everyone thinks there is inevitably going to be some degree of overbuild in this AI compute capacity gold rush. Just no agreement on how much. Will coreweave be this generations global crossing and nvidia this generations cisco. Or will it be less or more than the dark fiber overbuild. I think we'll soon find out. The difference this time around is that we have power as a fundamental limiting constraint, that we didnt have with dark fiber, and there capacity was also generated by the use of DWDM's to get more out of existing fiber which reduced the value of the new builds. Maybe we'll have something similar with AI compute with some new software or hardware innovation reducing the power and footprint needs. But regardless it now feels clear that these trees arent growing to the sky. They never do of course!!
