Hot on the heels of the administration forcing anthropic to pull back Fable and Mythos we had then advising OpenAI to only do a limited release of gpt-5.6 to a limited set of parties that they were to approve. Subsequently, Anthropic has been given permission to restart their project glasswing work, and apparently they will be allowed to rerelease Fable in the near future.
All of this feels like regulation to me, nothwithstanding the administrations position that its all voluntary. Hmm. Really.
In the meantime the chinese competition isnt slowing down and the administration doesnt seem to know what to do about that, including not taking a position about Anthropic's claims of massive distillation attacks by Alibaba and others. 
All of this is clearly leading to a very confused landscape, and certainly putting the use of US based LLM's by other nations at risk given the lack of trust there is now that the US administration might restrict access. Folks in Canada and Europe are now pushing ahead with moves towards sovereign AI as a result, which of course the US will try to influence but wont be able to control.

Lots of unintended consequences for sure. Going to be very intersting to see how all this evolves.
