Fuelling the furor about the cost of proprietary frontier models vs open models, we had two releases last week of "open" models that are purportedly close to frontier capabilities. Kimi K3 and Qwen-3.8 with GLM 5.5 due in August reported to be in the same class. What to do? Stop managing the anthropic and openai model releases and just compete openly, otherwise folks outside of the US will see the chinese models as a safer approach, especially as they reach "good enough" status. The speech by president Xi last week, positioning china as the safe, open option, is transparently a ploy to get control of the AI supply chain by servicing more demand (a play that has been used many times before in other industries), but if the US labs continue with their coy marketing approaches and the administration continues to intervene the chinese models will get momentum and potentially overtake the US models as they see more data and use cases. The warning shots have been fired.
