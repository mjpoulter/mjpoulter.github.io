Last week there was a lot of chatter about the news out of Google. Jeff Dean leaving with 3 high talent colleagues to start his own company, and Demis Hassabis being moved upstairs away from an operational role. The question is, is this the beginning of the end of Google/Deepmind as a credible frontier lab or just a reset to get them back on track. Feels like the recent in an attempt to get back in the game, especially as a leader in coding tools, as the Windsurf acquisition doesnt seem to have panned out quite as was expected, and Antigravity and the coding capabilities of the latest Gemini models is clearly lagging behind the frontier of Claude Code and OpenAI codex. Too early tell if the reset will work of course. Google have the heft and the access to talent but constantly come up against the priorities of their other services, search, ads, cloud etc.

On a related note, Meta's recent seems to be bearing fruit with muse spark, muse code and today muse glimmer an open weights model targetted at local execution. This gets them back to their credible fast follower position alongside the chinese labs and Space/X (who are soon to close the cursor acquisition).

Perhaps Google can run the same play and get back on their game. Time will tell.
