Ive got to admit i wasnt sure how the market would react to the Cerebras IPO. While all things AI are attracting investment, the folks competing against Nvidia are hard to value. Its not clear if they can keep their niche over the long term. Notwithstanding that concern, it seems the market wanted a piece of an Nvidia competitor. It will be interesting to see what happens to the stock price over the week as we get Nvidia's earnings on Wednesday and there is Google I/O as well that might have TPU announcements etc. But for now congratulations to Cerebras their wafer scale approach is genuinely innovative and if they can continue to exectute, perhaps they can keep a niche in the inference space. As always, time will tell.
