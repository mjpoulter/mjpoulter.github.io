It seems like demand is indeed growing at a crazy rate. We heard from Anthropic that they planned for 10x growth but ended up with 80x growth in the first quarter. So this has led to an active search for more compute capacity. They announced last week a couple of interesting deals. One with Akamai to use their edge compute cloud, which is an offering Akamai have been trying to develop to get traction.  Non-traditional servers, more attuned for the edge. But gpu capacity nonetheless. Im assuming they are planning to use this facility for inference at the edge.

The other deal was with Space/X to take over all the compute capacity in Colussus 1. Its the less reliable of the Colussus instalations, but it seems it was available and so Musk and Amodei seemed to have swallowed their differences to make a deal. Though Musk couldnt help saying he'd break the deal if Anthropic crossed over any of his red lines. With the Cursor deal/acquisition being based also on Colussus compute it seems Space/X in the run up to their IPO are trying to dress up the utilization which is apparently woefully low currently.

I think all these deals do indeed validate the demand, and we seem to be hitting the limits of what is currently available, so its going to be interesting to see how this all plays out in the next few months.
