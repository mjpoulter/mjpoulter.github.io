The debate continues around AI safety, but its clear that progress isnt really slowing, with a bunch of recent model releases. The administration isnt willing to allow the chinese to take the lead and the chinese arent willing to slow the competition. So i think for now we are going to need the frontier labs to follow Jensen Huang's urgings and regulate themselves. Only release when things are safe and aligned, regardless of what that does to competitive positioning of new capbilities. Then if things break we should introduce regulations as appropriate. Test and Learn. We'll see how that plays out!!
