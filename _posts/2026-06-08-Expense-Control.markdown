Well we dont have to worry about getting bored in the GenAI for software development space, or other spaces for that matter. We've gone from questioning the demand and the need for all the compute supply being built out, to now clearly having a compute shortage, rapidly increasing revenue for the frontier labs, IPO filings (anthropic and openai), equity and debt raises (google), and have moved on to concerns about the expense being incurred by enterprises looking to leverage the capability. Github's move last monday (which i almost blogged about) seeming to have raised the most ire.
So will the age old demand, supply equilibrium be reached soon, or will jeavons paradox continue to push demand. The folks beating that latter drum of course have a vested interest in seeing demand continue to rise, but if the costs increase to reflect the true cost of provisioning the compute, then maybe we do see a levelling off and a move to a more sustainable growth curve, like we saw with the cloud providers in their early days. Some folks are pushing the storyline that access to older (cheaper) models will meet the need of many use cases, but im not so sure that really drops costs much, because in most cases you can't really go back before say february this year and get the same level of effectiveness. eg.. maybe you can use opus 4.6 vs 4.8 and get a slightly lower cost for slightly lower efficacy.. but im not sure that really works out that well, especially if you need more turns of the crank to get to a good outcome.

Im thinking we'll start to move towards a better balance. But not immeadiately. As the models continue to improve we will indeed reach a point where the improvements are incremental and we dont need to jump on the latest model right away, but if the hype around mythos is true then we clearly arent there yet. And so folks really are going to have to start to be more disciplined about their spending, put quotas in place, do finops reviews (like we do for cloud) and start to take a usage based mindset to how we think about the space. The initial per seat, all you can eat buffet approach, that got us started is not going to be how this works going forward. Token expense management is going to be the new black art!!
Its going to be interesting to see how this plays out over the next quarter or so.
