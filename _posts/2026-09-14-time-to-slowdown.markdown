This weekend we had a flurry of calls, initiated by Dario Amodei, to slow the pace of LLM development, lest disaster strike as we lose control as the models move into RSI and bypass their controls. The call was supported to some extent or other by the heads of the other frontier labs, Openai, Google, Grok etc. But was met with less enthusiasm by the regulation sceptics, like David Sacks and the US administration, and notably by the Chinese, all of whom saw it as self-serving and a transparent attempt to lock in the advantage of the US based leading labs. Nvidia leader Jensen Huang saw it as a the proponents trying to create an atmosphere of fear in order to jin up demand for protective products/capabilities. Microsoft jumped in as a supporter on monday, but again laced with self-interest.

We'll see how all this plays out. But the genie is out of the bottle and its not possible to put it back in now and there is now incentive for those chasing the leaders to slow down. As Sacks suggested if they feel their are moving at an irresponsible pace its up to them to tap the brakes. Of course they prefer others to slow everyone down to preserve their lead. And that is unlikely to happen. More to come, no doubt.
