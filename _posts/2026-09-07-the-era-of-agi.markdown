There have been a bunch of new models release over the last few weeks. Some chinese open weights models with impressive capabilities, but noteably new releases from the frontier labs, Fable 5.1 from anthropic, gemini 3.8 flash from google and most notably gpt 6 - astra from openai. With this release Greg Brockman of OpenAI thinks we have entered the era of AGI. However his definition seems more than a little fuzzy.

Having said that the models are increasing in capability at a rapid pace, with many researchers now saying that things are moving faster than their ability to understand and control the model capabilities. How much of that is hype and marketing vs real concerns is hard to tell. I guess we need to see the impact as these new models are leveraged.

In other news Nvidia finally confirmed that they are acquiring HuggingFace, and declaring themselves the keepers of the flame of the open model ecosystem. Of course with a smidgeon of self-interest.

The build out of compute infrastructure continues apace, and outages of AI datacenter capacity, like a space/x colussus outage this week hit the news when it impacts the leading chat interfaces from openai, anthropic and space/x. It shows that "good enough" datacenter infrastructure is in the eye of the beholder. Good enough for training runs. Probably not for inference to paying customers. Powered datacenters to host the inference continues to be the bottleneck even as the model capabilities advance. The quest to find more efficient algorithms is taking second place to deploying more compute to support the current algorithms. It feels like more efficiency has to be found somewhere if this isnt going to get out of hand.

we will see.
